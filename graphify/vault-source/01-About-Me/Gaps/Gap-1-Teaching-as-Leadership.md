# Gap 1 Teaching as Leadership

*Teaching temperament not yet positioned as professional leadership*

External feedback describes Pol repeating procedures until the other person understands. Nowhere in the professional profile is this framed as a leadership capability. Reframe: 'I develop others' technical competency and use their understanding as the metric of success.'
