# Portfolio Narrative

Predoctoral scientist based in Barcelona, working at the intersection of translational neuroscience, nanomedicine, and lysosomal biology. The throughline: can a therapy actually reach the cells that need it, and can that reach change a patient's life?

Origin story: started in a liver-disease internship (NAFLD, VHIR), which redirected toward the Autophagy and Lysosomal Dysfunction Group in 2023 — a pivot that became the Master's thesis (VHIP patient phenotyping) and then the PhD topic.

Note: the portfolio site (index.html) and CV use the phrase "nanoparticle-based drug delivery" for Pol's own PhD research track. This is a distinct research thread from NanoERT the company, whose platform is specifically an IV-administered, biopolymer-conjugated enzyme (see [[Platform Mechanism]]). Keep these two threads distinguished in the graph even though they share the same underlying lysosomal-disease domain.
