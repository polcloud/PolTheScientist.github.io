# LinkedIn Voice Style Guide

Extracted from the `pol-linkedin-voice` skill and cross-validated against the 14-15 real published posts and the strategy guide's post analysis.

## Two voices, never mixed in one post
- **COMPANY (C)** — "We," authoritative, institutional
- **PERSONAL (P)** — "I," reflective, honest about uncertainty

## Structural DNA (applies to every post)
1. Three-beat opening — three short lines before the fold, building tension
2. Bolded refutation — 1-2 short bolded lines reframing the problem after the hook
3. Bold at clause/sentence level, not just keywords
4. 📌 opens any rhetorical question or topic pivot
5. 🔹 reserved for bullets under a 📌 header
6. ☣️ marks the pivot into consequence — at most once
7. ‼️ exactly once per post, at the single hardest fact
8. 💙 precedes every human-cost anchor and every bridge
9. 🎯 always opens the bridge back to NanoERT/Pol's own work — states preclinical stage, never promotional
10. Arrow chains: ➡️ for mechanistic cascades, `-->` for narrative cause-effect
11. Closing triplet: two short noun-phrase lines, then one longer resolving line, ending in 💙
12. 💬 always opens the CTA
13. Unicode bold only, never mixed with italics
14. Hashtags: 6-13 total, brand tag(s) included, 2-4 embedded inline mid-body

## Five-part post structure
1. HOOK (1 line) — 2. CONTEXT (60-100 words) — 3. BODY/EVIDENCE (100-200 words) — 4. BRIDGE (40-60 words) — 5. CTA + HASHTAGS

Total: 200-450 words.
