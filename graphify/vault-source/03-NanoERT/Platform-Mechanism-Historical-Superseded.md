# Platform Mechanism Historical Superseded

**Superseded — kept for history, not for content generation.**

Earlier materials (Pol's CV, portfolio site, the original Profile Graph document, the first `NanoERT_LinkedIn_Strategy_Guide.docx` calendar for weeks 16-52, and the original `complete-linkedin-post` skill) described NanoERT's platform as:
- "Intranasal nanoparticle-mediated enzyme delivery"
- Bypassing the blood-brain barrier via "olfactory and trigeminal nerve pathways"
- A general "nanoparticle-based drug delivery" system

Pol confirmed (this conversation) that NanoERT has never been intranasal — it is IV-only. The `complete-linkedin-post` skill has been corrected to remove this language. The old weeks-16-52 calendar (with posts on intranasal "nose-to-brain" delivery, mRNA-ERT, and lipid nanoparticles) is superseded by the current [[NanoERT LinkedIn Calendar 2026-2027]] and should not be treated as current fact.
