# WORLD Symposium Pau Results

We want to highlight the impressive participation of Pau Sarle Valles at the leading conference of lysosomal storage diseases: WORLD Symposium 2026.

At the congress, Pau delivered a tailored presentation on "Next-generation enzyme replacement strategies for Gaucher Disease." Did he deliver the pitch? We received inspiring feedback from field experts, clinicians, and promising collaborators, confirming: the quality and expertise of our professional team, and the relevance and translational potential of our approach.

At NanoERT, our mission is to restore healthy lysosomal function and accelerate advanced enzyme and nanobiotechnological platforms towards the clinic. However, to do so we need to reach people with a cutting-edge and understandable therapy — which Pau's contribution at WORLD certainly achieved.

Next up: we'll share key scientific insights to shape the future of lysosomal and rare disease therapies.
