# NanoERT Reaches La Vanguardia

NanoERT reaches La Vanguardia! We're thrilled to see this spotlight on the work behind NanoERT Therapeutics: "Aiming to improve patients' lives through cutting-edge nanomedicine: transforming ERT for Gaucher and rare diseases without effective treatments."

At NanoERT, we celebrate every innovation that gets us closer to better, more accessible treatments for rare diseases. We congratulate Julia Lorenzo Rivera and Sebastian Tanco, and the collaborative work of IBB UAB, shown in the Vanguardia coverage.

This achievement is: a step forward towards the consolidation and implementation of NanoERT's next-generation therapy in hospitals; a powerful reminder of why we work at the intersection of biotech, protein engineering, and patient-centered innovation; a recognition that strengthens our commitment to bring this science from the lab to life — to clinics, and most importantly, to patients who need it most.

Hashtags: #InnovationInHealth #PatientFirst #ScienceToPatients #BiotechForGood #Nanomedicine #NeurodegenerativeDiseases #GaucherDisease
