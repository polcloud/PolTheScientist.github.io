# GBA in Numbers Repeat Version

A second, closely related version of the "Were we wrong with Parkinson's disease" post (per the strategy guide's post inventory, this is listed as a distinct repeat/second version emphasizing the GBA-in-numbers epidemiology data). Same core facts: GBA1 as the #1 PD genetic risk factor, ~10-12% prevalence in idiopathic PD (~25% in Ashkenazi Jewish populations), and convergence of PD risk genes (LRRK2, VPS35, ATP13A2, CTSD) on endolysosomal pathways.
