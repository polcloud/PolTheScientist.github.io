# Parkinsons Day Science Means Nothing Without People

Science means nothing without people. As scientists working on advanced nanotherapies for Parkinson's disease and lysosomal storage disorders, it's easy to get caught up in data and protocols.

NanoERT's presence and dedication at the 4th Annual Parkinson's Day at Vall d'Hebron Hospital couldn't go unnoticed. We collaborated with organizer Ariadna Laguna Tuset to explain our research to interested patients and families, aiming to bring the bench closer to the final users of our technology.

Highlights from the day: Dr. Victoria Gonzalez emphasized the urgent need for robust patient registries, especially in early-onset cases, to accelerate personalized medicine. Dr. Silvia Enriquez Calzada introduced PARKEX and PAIR, two projects showing the power of movement, exercising, mentoring, and social connection in Parkinson's care.

Take-home messages: from Emilia Duran's encouraging speech on living with PD and how initiatives like Parkex improved her wellbeing — "Movement is medicine." From patient associations — "We can reach milestones beyond our beliefs." From Vall Hebron hospital professionals — "We are on the right track but we are not there yet."

For NanoERT, these events are the engine of our work. Every voice heard fuels our commitment to transform neurodegenerative disease treatment from the inside out. Thank you to Emilia Duran, Vall d'Hebron, the Catalan Parkinson's Association, and all participants who made this a space of empathy, knowledge, and shared purpose.
