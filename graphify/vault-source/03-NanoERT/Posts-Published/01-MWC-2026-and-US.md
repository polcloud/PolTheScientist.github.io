# MWC 2026 and US

NanoERT Therapeutics attended MWC (Mobile World Congress) 2026 in Barcelona — one of the world's most influential innovation ecosystems.

While the event is globally recognized for shaping the future of connectivity, AI, and digital technologies, it also represents a convergence point where deep technology meets life sciences.

Why attend: positioning rare disease innovation within the global tech ecosystem; rare and neurodegenerative diseases remain among the largest unmet medical challenges in modern medicine; the future of medicine will be built at the intersection of biotechnology, deep tech, and global innovation networks.

Sebastian Tanco, one of NanoERT's lead scientists, represented the company: exchanged insights with key opinion leaders and innovators, explored collaborations with investors and technology partners, and mapped how next-generation platforms can accelerate therapeutic development.
