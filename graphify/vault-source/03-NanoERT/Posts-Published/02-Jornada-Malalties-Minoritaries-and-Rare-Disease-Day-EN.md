# Jornada Malalties Minoritaries and Rare Disease Day EN

What do Gaucher disease and Parkinson's disease have in common? More than you might think — and it shows why rare disease research matters.

NanoERT's team attended the "Jornada de Malalties Minoritàries" at Hospital Universitari Vall d'Hebron, where patients, clinicians, researchers, and advocates gathered to raise awareness and advance the future of rare disease care.

Two statistics stood out: +42,000 people live with rare diseases in the region; +50% are children.

One of the most powerful moments was the patient roundtable, where patients and families shared their journeys navigating diagnosis and treatment. Patients must be part of key decision-making processes in research, healthcare systems, and therapy development.

"Small numbers. Big impact." Although one rare disease affects a relatively small population, the associated impact of their research extends to more prevalent diseases — e.g., research in Gaucher disease linked mutations in the GBA gene to developing Parkinson's disease, its first genetic risk factor.
