# PhD Celebration Eddie Pradas Gracia

A proud moment for our team! NanoERT celebrates colleague Dr. Eddie Pradas Gracia, who successfully defended his PhD thesis titled "Development of new protein nanoconjugation strategies to improve enzyme replacement therapy."

His groundbreaking work focuses on neurodegenerative diseases and rare diseases around Gaucher disease, aiming to bring disease-modifying therapies to patients. For NanoERT, Eddie's success is not only a personal milestone but a shared victory for the team, strengthening the collective mission: bringing innovation, hope, and better therapies to patients who need them most.

We are proud of you, Eddie — what an achievement! We hope to keep you by our side as we continue pushing the boundaries of science together.
