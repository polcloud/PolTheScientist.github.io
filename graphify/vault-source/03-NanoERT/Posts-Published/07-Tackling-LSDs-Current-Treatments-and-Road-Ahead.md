# Tackling LSDs Current Treatments and Road Ahead

Tackling Lysosomal Storage Diseases: Current Treatments & the Road Ahead.

Over the last two decades, several therapeutic approaches have emerged. Enzyme Replacement Therapy continues to stand out as the most established and transformative.

1. Enzyme Replacement Therapy (ERT): regular infusions of a recombinant version of the missing enzyme, taken up by cells and delivered to lysosomes to restore normal degradation. Strengths: proven clinical benefits (organ volume reduction, improved mobility, better quality of life), directly replaces the missing enzyme, established for multiple LSDs (Gaucher, Fabry, Pompe, MPS I/II/IV/VI/VII). Limitations: limited access to the brain (doesn't cross the blood-brain barrier), requires lifelong periodic intravenous infusions, potential immune reactions.

2. Substrate Reduction Therapy (SRT): reduces synthesis of the accumulated substrate.
3. Pharmacological Chaperone Therapy (PCT): small molecules stabilize mutant enzymes, helping them fold and reach lysosomes.
4. Hematopoietic Stem Cell Transplantation (HSCT): donor stem cells produce functional enzyme that migrates to tissues.

Emerging therapies: gene therapy (corrects the genetic defect at source), next-gen ERTs (improved enzyme engineering, targeted delivery), and combination approaches (ERT + SRT or ERT + chaperones).
