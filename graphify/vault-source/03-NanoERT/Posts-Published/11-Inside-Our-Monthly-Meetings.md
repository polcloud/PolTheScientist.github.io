# Inside Our Monthly Meetings

Inside Our Monthly Meetings: Aligning Discovery with Delivery.

In biotech, the science is hard — but the alignment is demanding. We're building a nanotechnology platform to deliver recombinant lysosomal enzymes to hard-to-reach organs, including the brain. Our targets are diseases like Gaucher disease and Parkinson's disease, where lysosomal dysfunction plays a central role — and where conventional ERT falls short.

Every month, our team gathers to do something deceptively simple: keep our platform grounded in results, and pointed towards the clinic. Two critical objectives: (1) tracking progress across R&D subteams, surfacing achievements; (2) redirecting fast — when bottlenecks happen, we resolve them in real time, propose alternatives, and update experimental priorities.

Structure: Part 1, Data-Driven Discussion — we present our latest experimental outcomes and evaluate whether our data still supports our vision of precision, safety, market viability. Part 2, Strategic Review — we cover regulatory positioning, startup milestones (e.g., non-dilutive funding), and business readiness. When deeper focus is needed, we launch topic-specific follow-ups with only key experts in the room.

This month, we didn't kill a pathway — but we validated a direction. How do you structure internal meetings to balance science and speed?
