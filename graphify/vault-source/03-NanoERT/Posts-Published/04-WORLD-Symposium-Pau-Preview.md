# WORLD Symposium Pau Preview

NanoERT at WORLD Symposium: Bringing Lysosomal Science Closer to Patients.

Pau Sarle Valles will be representing the group at WORLD Symposium — the leading international congress dedicated to lysosomal biology, rare diseases, and translational innovation.

Pau's participation: a poster presentation, a roundtable discussion, and sharing expertise in recombinant human lysosomal protein production, advanced purification strategies, and rigorous quality control and characterization of therapeutic-grade enzymes.

These are the cornerstones of Enzyme Replacement Therapy (ERT) and of any successful translation to the clinic. High-quality, scalable, and well-characterized lysosomal enzymes are essential to ensure safety, efficacy, and regulatory readiness.

Pau's work directly supports NanoERT's efforts in Gaucher disease and the optimization of manufacturing processes for other lysosomal proteins linked to lysosomal storage disorders and neurodegenerative diseases.
