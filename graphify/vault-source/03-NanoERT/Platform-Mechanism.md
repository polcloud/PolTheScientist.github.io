# Platform Mechanism

**Canonical, current (confirmed by Pol).**

NanoERT's delivery system is an **intravenously administered enzyme replacement therapy (IV-ERT)** in which the therapeutic enzyme is **conjugated to a biopolymer**. This is designed to:
- Increase circulating stability and half-life
- Reduce immune recognition / anti-drug antibody (ADA) formation
- Improve delivery to disease-relevant tissue (e.g., bone in Gaucher, kidney/heart in Fabry)

This is **not** intranasal delivery, **not** olfactory/trigeminal-pathway BBB bypass, and **not** nanoparticle encapsulation in the LNP/carrier sense. Confirmed directly in a real published post ("Understanding Gaucher Disease"): *"Our proprietary technology conjugates a recombinant therapeutical enzyme to a biopolymer, engineered to: improve enzyme stability, extend half-life in circulation, enhance targeting of affected tissues (including bone), deliver superior pharmacodynamic performance compared to standard ERT."*

Independently corroborated by a public VHIR/UAB IBB news item (27 May 2025) describing the underlying academic project as "nanoconjugación enzima-polímero de GBA (NANO-ERT)," co-developed by the Protein Engineering and Nanomedicine Group (IBB-UAB), VHIR, and CIPF.

Always stated as **preclinical stage** — no clinical data, timelines, or efficacy claims.
