# Pipeline GBA

**GBA / glucocerebrosidase (GCase) — Gaucher disease, GBA-Parkinson's disease**

GBA gene mutations impair glucocerebrosidase (GCase) activity, causing glucocerebroside accumulation in macrophages and Gaucher cell formation. GBA1 variants are the most common genetic risk factor for Parkinson's disease (~10-12% of idiopathic PD patients; up to ~25% of Ashkenazi Jewish PD patients), linked to faster progression, earlier onset, and earlier cognitive decline. GBA and CTSD form a mechanistically validated feedback loop with alpha-synuclein clearance.
