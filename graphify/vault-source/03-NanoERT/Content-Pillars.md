# Content Pillars

Six-pillar rotation (no more than 3 Company posts in a row without a Personal post):

### Science Visible
Mechanistic/expert explanation — GBA-CTSD-alpha-synuclein axis, Gb3, GCase, autophagy.

### Patient Reality
Patient/caregiver-centred — diagnostic delay, unmet need, human cost, patient event reflections.

### Platform Introduction
The biopolymer-conjugated IV-ERT technology and pipeline status.

### Sector Intelligence
Regulatory, funding, competitor watch (Azafaros, Denali, orphan drug frameworks).

### Human Behind Science
Pol's personal researcher voice — lab reflections, PhD life, spin-off mindset.

### Frontier Watch
Diseases OUTSIDE NanoERT's active pipeline (Alzheimer's, Huntington's, MPS III, MPS II) — always explicitly labeled exploratory/non-pipeline, third-party public science only.

### Event Milestone
Conference attendance, posters, press, team achievements.
