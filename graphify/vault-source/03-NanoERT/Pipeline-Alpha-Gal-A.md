# Pipeline Alpha-Gal A

**Alpha-galactosidase A — Fabry disease**

GLA gene mutations cause alpha-galactosidase A deficiency, leading to Gb3 (globotriaosylceramide) accumulation across kidney, heart, skin, and nerves. Diagnostic delay averages 10-15 years. Conventional ERT lowers Gb3 but often fails to fully halt cardiac (LVH, fibrosis) and renal organ damage once established.
